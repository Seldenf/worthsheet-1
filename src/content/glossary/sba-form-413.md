For SBA 7(a) and 504 loans and surety bonds, the form is completed for each proprietor, general partner, LLC managing member, each owner of 20% or more of the business and anyone guaranteeing the loan, and returned to the lender, Certified Development Company or surety rather than to the SBA ([SBA Form 413](https://www.sba.gov/document/sba-form-413-personal-financial-statement)). It lists assets, liabilities, income sources and [contingent liabilities](/glossary/contingent-liabilities/) as of one date, and [net worth](/glossary/net-worth/) is total assets minus total liabilities.

Lenders use it to judge the owners' personal strength alongside the business's. The current edition (05-24) says the information must be current within 120 days of submission for 7(a), 504, surety bond, 8(a) and WOSB applications (90 days for Disaster). Lenders may ask for fresher numbers, so follow the lender's instructions on timing.

Form 413 is a [personal financial statement](/glossary/personal-financial-statement/), but a PFS is a general idea and Form 413 is one specific form. For 7(a), 504 and surety purposes the SBA says other formats with the same data may be accepted, so ask the lender which to use. For a full walkthrough see [how to fill out SBA Form 413](/how-to-fill-out-sba-form-413/), and for the loan program see [SBA 7(a)](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans/7a-loans).
